❓ Help / Tips
• Expressive speech: write emotion hints inside the text, like: (laughing) No way! or [مستعجل] اركب بسرعة — the AI acts them. Both ( ) and [ ] work.
• Arabic: just type Arabic text, it works.
• Three engines: ⭐ Puter is the recommended free one — reliable OpenAI voices that act [مستعجل] emotion tags; it asks you to create a free account once (no card). Gemini acts emotions best but has a small daily quota. Pollinations needs nothing but is often busy.
• Effects (robot, ghost, devil...) are applied on your device after the AI speaks, so they are 100% free. The Pitch / Echo / Grit sliders stack on top of any preset.
• 🎤 Record: every line also has a Record button — record your own voice with the microphone and all the same effects and sliders work on it. No AI, no quota, no internet needed.
• 🌊 Waveform editing: after generating or recording, drag on the wave to select a part, then ✂️ Trim keeps only the selection, 🗑️ Cut removes it and joins the rest (splice out a mistake), ✃ Split breaks the line into two at your tap point, and 🔇 Auto-trim silence removes quiet gaps at the start and end. ▶ Selection previews the picked part, and ↶ Undo reverses any edit.
• 🩹 Clean-up (recordings): Fix clicks/pops finds sudden sharp spikes (mic bumps, mouth clicks) and smooths them out automatically. For background hiss: select a flat, quiet part of the wave with no talking, press 📎 Learn noise, then 🧹 Remove noise to filter that hiss out of the whole line.
• If MP3 download fails (no internet for the encoder), you automatically get a WAV file instead — also works in all game engines.
• Quota saving: already-generated lines are skipped, three different models are tried automatically (each has its own free limit), and 💰 ONE-request mode makes the whole conversation for the price of a single line.
• Free plan limit: if you see "quota" errors, wait 1-2 minutes and try again — the daily limit resets every night.
