❓ Help / Tips
• Expressive speech: write emotion hints inside the text, like: (laughing) No way! or [مستعجل] اركب بسرعة — the AI acts them. Both ( ) and [ ] work.
• Arabic: just type Arabic text, it works.
• Effects (robot, ghost, kid...) are applied on your device after the AI speaks, so they are 100% free.
• If MP3 download fails (no internet for the encoder), you automatically get a WAV file instead — also works in all game engines.
• Quota saving: already-generated lines are skipped, three different models are tried automatically (each has its own free limit), and 💰 ONE-request mode makes the whole conversation for the price of a single line.
• Free plan limit: if you see "quota" errors, wait 1-2 minutes and try again — the daily limit resets every night.
